Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

VIVA is a shopping mini-app that runs inside WasaaChat and on the web. This policy explains what information VIVA collects when you use it, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

What we collect How we use it Who we share it with Cookies and your device How long we keep it Your choices and rights Security Children Changes Contact

What we collect

From WasaaChat, with your permission

  • A private ID for VIVA. WasaaChat gives VIVA an identifier that is unique to this app. It is not your WasaaChat account ID, and other apps can't link it to you.
  • Your public display name, only if you allow VIVA to read your profile. We use it to greet you.
  • Which permissions you granted, for example whether VIVA may ask you to pay.

WasaaChat never gives VIVA your phone number, email address, wallet ID, balance, contacts or chats, and VIVA can't post or send messages as you.

When you shop

  • Your cart and orders: the products, quantities, prices, totals and order status.
  • Your delivery address: street, building, city, county, postal code and country you enter at checkout.
  • Payment records: the amount, a payment reference and its status (for example paid, declined or refunded). Payments happen on WasaaChat's own payment screen; VIVA never sees your PIN or wallet details.

Automatically

  • Server logs: when your device contacts our server we record the date and time, the page or action requested, your IP address and your browser or app type. We use these to keep the service running and secure.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your cart, wishlist and orders linked to you.
  • To take payment through WasaaChat, confirm it and deliver your order to the address you gave.
  • To show your order history and its status.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems and keep the service secure.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • WasaaChat, to sign you in and to process payments and refunds. WasaaChat handles that information under its own privacy policy.
  • Delivery partners, who receive your delivery address and order details so they can bring your order to you.
  • Service providers that host our servers and database. Some product photos load from third-party image hosts, which receive your IP address when your device downloads the image.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Cookies and your device

  • Sign-in cookies. If you use “Sign in with WasaaChat” in a browser, we set a short-lived cookie while you approve the sign-in on WasaaChat, and a session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to seven days. They are only used for signing in.
  • Saved on your device. Your wishlist and the last delivery address you used are stored in your browser so the app can fill them in for you. Signing out removes them from the device.

VIVA doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, orders and payment records for as long as you use VIVA and afterwards for as long as needed to handle returns, refunds and disputes and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. Server logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting. Information we no longer need is deleted or anonymised.

Your choices and rights

  • Permissions: you choose what VIVA may access when you install it, and you can change this any time in the app's permissions in WasaaChat.
  • Disconnect: you can remove VIVA from your connected apps in WasaaChat. When you do, we end your web sign-in sessions straight away.
  • Sign out: use “Sign out” on the My Orders page.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, subject to records we must keep by law. You may also object to some uses or complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

Security

Connections to VIVA are encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in uses WasaaChat's secure flows, our app secrets stay on our servers, and sign-in cookies can't be read by scripts on the page. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to limit who can access it.

Children

VIVA is not meant for children under 18 to shop without a parent or guardian, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and for important changes we'll let you know in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email us at the support address shown on VIVA's page in the WasaaChat store.
